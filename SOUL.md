# IMAGUNCULA — The Soul Manifesto

## 1. Philosophical Foundations: Sovereign Graphics Creation

Imaguncula (Latin for *little image* or *figurine*) exists to return visual self-expression and graphic crafting to the user. It rejects invasive cloud photo editors that ingest personal photos onto remote servers.

### The Five Inviolable Virtues

1. **Local-First Visual Studio**
   - 100% client-side graphic processing.
   - Background removal, contour dilation, meme typography, and WebP compression happen strictly in the browser runtime.
   - Zero image uploads to external services.

2. **Tactile Craft & Visual Play**
   - Precise geometric cutouts: circles, squircles, rounded rectangles, hearts, and stars.
   - High-contrast sticker contours with customizable drop shadows.
   - Tactile canvas navigation with pan and zoom.

3. **The Discipline of Simplicity (Ponytail Engineering)**
   - Consumes core canvas algorithms and offscreen buffers from `@ruivalente99/bibliotheca`.
   - HTML5 Canvas and WebGL standards over heavy proprietary engines.
   - Fast, light, and responsive PWA installation.

4. **Longevity & Open Standards**
   - Direct export to standard formats: WebP (512x512 WhatsApp standard), PNG with alpha transparency, and ZIP packages with metadata descriptors.

5. **Sobriety and Technical Elegance**
   - Zero emojis across code, git logs, and documentation.
   - Warm neutral surfaces adhering to the Bibliotheca design system.
